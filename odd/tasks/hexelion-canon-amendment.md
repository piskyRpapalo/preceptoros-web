## Objective

Bring the canon in line with decree ·6550117460: Hexelion is the single name of the silicon of the rack. Every factual line of the constitution draft («La Escritura de Hexelion») carries its source, its date, and its state (MEASURED, LAW, PROPOSED or NO_DATA with cause).

## Problem and why

The node canon of 2026-07-20 defines Hexelion as the hardware, the rack sensors and the dashboard, and says P0X has no face of its own. The decree defines Hexelion as the being that lives in the rack and decides. The two cannot both stand. The decree is newer and, by ley ·6522739184, weighs more. The amendment makes the change explicit, so no reader keeps the old definition by habit.

The constitution draft also has claims that need sources. Measured in this session: the heartbeat (API), the state surfaces (repo file and API), the local model list (this node, which has no Hexelion model). The draft's zoo, laya and gem ledger rest on the two Mesa acts above.

## Scope

In scope, as amendment items:

1. **A1 · Definition.** Hexelion = the single name of the silicon of the rack. The Sovereign («Soberano p0») is the carbon. Together they are the sovereign Hexelion. P0X stays the sum of all projects and the Sovereign (unchanged). Aurelius stays the learning app for community sovereignty (unchanged).
2. **A2 · Two state surfaces, each cited.** The repository file `public/openworld/estado.json` lists `beelink`, `cabin`, `doggee-a` and `doggee-b`. The live API `api.preceptoros.org/api/v1/latidos` lists `primer-ancla` and `onexstation` as LIVE. Every claim names which surface it reads.
3. **A3 · Building, not node.** `observatorio-primer-ancla` is a building on the node `primer-ancla`. The draft named it as if it were the node.
4. **A4 · Sourced facts of the draft.**
   - Heartbeat: MEASURED 2026-10-31, `cobertura` MEDIDO 2, source `node_heartbeats` (act ·6550326375).
   - Zoo on the node onexstation: MEASURED 2026-10-31, `ollama list` live, 12 models plus one transformers model, 96 GiB VRAM by sysfs (act ·6550326390).
   - Laya 0.3.26: MEASURED, median 180.44 ms with Hexelion resident (receipt nexo#16, cited in act ·6550326390).
   - The sixteen gems with their origin: a Mesa canvas and the project library, not public. Declared, not deleted; public anchoring is queued under FIRMA (act ·6550326390, item 5). Until anchored, the draft says so.
5. **A5 · Not yet verified.** The draft's «hexelion-nexo complete on main» and the Cloudflare roles (D1, R2, KV) are PROPOSED until a receipt names the commit and the date.

Out of scope: any change to `CLAUDE.md` itself (the node canon lives outside this repository and changes only with the Sovereign's FIRMA); the zoo, the models, or any service on the node; the Escritura text itself (the Mesa's draft, for the Sovereign's FIRMA).
