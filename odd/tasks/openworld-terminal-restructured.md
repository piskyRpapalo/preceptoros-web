ARCHITECTURE (this replaces the earlier two-halves draft). The map is the showcase. Content panels open as leaves over the showcase, one at a time, and do not bury it. Communication is not a fixed half of the screen: it is a terminal that materialises in the lower centre, in the gap between the two joysticks, with the look of a 2000s mp3 player (a winamp-style strip). Desktop and phone both read this way; on phone the open leaf takes the full content area and the winamp stays the lower strip between the joystick corners.

P1 · Welcome — the showcase's front leaf. Two things only: a non-technical sentence of what the map is (AI models running on equipment their owner controls, under a human protocol; nodes raise buildings with creative freedom for whoever owns them; you, the ephemeral visitor, interact while you are live), and the live state — how many nodes are live and how many ephemeral accounts are live. The node count is read, not written: it lives in the open-world state (only LIVE/STALE are drawn; the served line is the "your cabin is awake · N more places still asleep" form). The ephemeral-account count is NO_DATA with cause — the functional ephemeral account is phase 1 of the ephemeral-user commission and is not yet a countable, movable unit. The non-technical sentence extends the front door's own register ("a world of waves that runs on your own device. No account, no cloud, no tracking"); it does not coin a new voice.

P2 · Map — deleted as a leaf. Its content moves to Welcome: the live node state, and the walkers as presence/count, not as the raw trail served today ("walker of cabin: building · at cabin · stops cabin → …"), which the fold rule keeps in Technical details. Map control is the joysticks (below). The "Walk to cabin / Walk to primer-ancla / Walk to onexstation / Walk to doggee-s110" buttons stop being the navigation surface; walking is touch (a tap on a hex walks, a tap on the cabin enters) and the joysticks.

P3 · Tutorial — deleted as a leaf. The three choices — Tutorial / Free walk / Skip tutorial — appear at the first signature, when the visitor signs on the device and the ephemeral account is created (the "Play now · sign on this device" moment). All three already exist and are seeked, not rewritten: the six-step walk (key → beacon +100 copper → summon egg → hatch → fight the Reef patrol +50 → choose a node, with "Stay here for now"), always skippable, skipping keeping what you have and leaving the rewards unearned; free walk as a tap outside a node; the skip. Codex seeks the written flow and mounts it on the signature, not as a panel.

P4 + P5 · house-summons and army — merged into one leaf, "profile" (a human-head icon), and postponed until the ephemeral account opens. On signature, the ephemeral account's HTML is generated with its resources and its army. Everything is already written and seeked: the house-is-your-crab state that lives in the terminal, the copper resources, and the wave/summon pieces vendored and sha-pinned on the app side (the first wave duel is plan-only; the node-army phase is not started — plan first, no code before its sign-off). Profile is the ephemeral account's sheet; it waits for the account, exactly as the character joystick waits for a unit to move.

P6 · Radio → the ephemeral chat — the main communication leaf, and the winamp. Investigate first what the radio already says: today it is the program that narrates the map (the served log is the "Map · Nodes online: …" stream — the state the radio carries, the note that never lies). Then make the radio the ear of the map for a blind visitor: the same map events the eye reads, spoken — the same decision as the parser/A2A surface, one fact rendered for eye, ear, haptic and parser. The leaf, opened, materialises as the winamp strip in the lower centre between the joysticks, without covering the showcase. Physically it holds the three functions that already live there — Echoes, Chat, Radio (the sub-tabs served today) — and the chat it carries is the ephemeral chat the project promised for years and could not run for lack of infrastructure maturity, now that the way exists: the radio log, the map chat, and the web node brain that works and speaks (the first live link). Any fourth function in the winamp is NO_DATA until further notice.

P7 · Models — deleted as a leaf of the map. The models and the downloads move to the first message, into the "Tools" interactive. Tools becomes a one-click offer list: what can be downloaded from the web (the web's PWA — the installable manifest; the app package, NO_DATA today; the LoRA; the Hugging Face links), each item compressed to file name + size + a one-line description. What cannot be downloaded from the web is the rest, declared NO_DATA with an explanation, not hidden — because the web is offline by law and makes zero external requests on load, so downloads live behind an explicit user action (Tools), never on map load. The model cards served today (the seven frames, residency NO_DATA, the web-node-brain paragraph, "download and verify it", the companion-context NO_DATA line) are the content Tools renders; the brains catalogue is the registered source of truth, seeked, not re-authored.

CROSS-CUTTING GESTURES (kept from the agreed shape, now placed on this architecture):

Framing move. A click on a building, node or interactive runs the deterministic camera to frame the two graphic units — the visitor's character and the clicked entity — so together they fill the visible showcase, then the leaf rises from the bottom into the content area. The worm decorates the switch; the camera frames (they are two engines, not one). Desktop: the framed strip is the top ~20% and the leaf the remaining ~80%, the map compressed just under the territory name. Phone: the leaf takes 100% of the content area — the carbon still sees the transition, without losing reading quality (what the eye follows does not shrink; nameplates and seals read at any zoom). The precedent is measured: Explore already lifts the canvas to ≥80% with Escape back. Reduced motion snaps quiet.

Two joysticks, lower corners of the showcase. One drives the map view (the pan and the perspective that today only two fingers on Android reach); one drives the visitor's character across the hexes. A joystick is pointer-agnostic — a drag is a drag whether the pointer is a mouse or a finger — so the PC perspective problem dies with the same control that serves Android, and the six-direction pad, the seven controls that compete with entering, and the arrow-walk all go to the corners these two occupy. The pad was retired on paper yet the served capture still shows it: confirm against HEAD which is live before deleting, so the piece closes the gap instead of assuming it. Today only the view joystick lands, because there is no ephemeral unit to move yet; the character joystick waits for that unit, and when it arrives it is the physical driver of the swap below. This base is also what later lets the lane build wave combat and wave summon; those live in the private house and are not built here — the joysticks open the door, the waves walk through it later, under their own sign-off, in their own repo. The arrow-walk was the keyboard spine; its removal is recorded as the Sovereign's decision, not a silent drop, and the no-joystick visitor is still served by tap-to-engage and the radio.

Ephemeral swap. The ephemeral account is the panel selector: when it enters and engages a map interactive, the leaves re-point to that interactive's datum — one account, one interlocutor at a time, one leaf that follows it, the way entering a colony node already adopts that node's colours. The anchors that bind an interactive to its building live in the structure the Descent is building; Codex wires the swap, not the anchors.
