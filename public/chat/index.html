Talk to Hexelion

You are talking to Hexelion, an AI on the Soberano's node. It advises; it never signs.

    Sending starts a visit: a key made on this device that ends when you leave. Your chat stays only on this device.

    Technical details

    Nothing is requested when this page opens. Each Send is one request to api.preceptoros.org/api/v1/chat; the door passes it to Hexelion's room and you never reach the node directly.

    A turn is under 1 KB (text up to 600 characters) and the answer is a bubble under 1,024 B. Limits: 6 turns a minute per visit, 30 per network, 40 per visit.

    What travels is a hash of your visit key, the text and the turn counter with its seal. The door keeps nothing; your turns live in this browser's IndexedDB until you forget them.

    When Hexelion cannot answer you see NO_DATA with its cause: asleep, not taking visitors, too slow, out of step, or limited.
