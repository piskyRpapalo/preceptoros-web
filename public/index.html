A world of waves that runs on your own device. No account, no cloud, no tracking.

Welcome to OpenWorld

A shared isometric world drawn only in waves. Every building is a door to something real: a model you can run, a chat, a workshop, a duel. Nothing here is drawn by hand; each creature is computed from its own harmonics.

Create your signature on this device (the key never leaves it), then walk the map, enter buildings, talk to the guides and summon your first troop.
