Technical details

The checker reads preceptoros.cedula-nodo/1 under 1 KiB and verifies its integrity hash. Its authenticity stays NO_DATA without a trust anchor. When the registry is live, it will answer with a receipt this page checks by recomputing its hash; a receipt is not a signature.
