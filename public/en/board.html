There is one task: finish PreceptorOS's story mode on your machine and write your comment in the Benchmark. That is all you are asked for. Everything else on this board —challenges, shared compute, general threads— comes from people who already did it.

How this works

  • The challenge is the engine. Someone says which measurement is missing. Whoever has the hardware measures it and signs the line. That is how the Benchmark grows: by named gaps, not by commissions.
  • Publishing is opt-in, and one file stops it. Your PreceptorOS only sends what you ask it to send. And to cut it at the root, sanctuary mode is an empty file called MODO_SANTUARIO: it overrides whatever your configuration says and does not rewrite it. Delete it and everything is as it was.
  • These threads are examples. There is no community yet. Faking activity in an empty forum is the oldest lie on the internet, so it is declared above rather than hidden.
