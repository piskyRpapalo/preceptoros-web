My profile
What is here and what is not
  • The pseudonym is derived, not chosen. It comes from the SHA-256 of your public key. Changing your name means changing your key, and changing your key means starting over: that is the price of nobody being able to impersonate you without a server preventing it.
  • The three levels are not a game. There are no points and no badges: a level here is exactly what you can do that you could not do before. Yours comes from looking at two things on this device —whether there is a key and whether there are corrected pairs stored—, not from a counter anyone keeps.
  • This page publishes nothing on its own. There is now somewhere to send your key —the rack's Agora— but it only leaves if you press the button above, and it leaves signed by you. Nothing is sent on load.
