What is here and what is not

  • The pseudonym is derived, not chosen. It comes from the SHA-256 of your public key. Changing your name means changing your key, and changing your key means starting over: that is the price of nobody being able to impersonate you without a server preventing it.
  • What you write stays in your browser. The biography is stored in IndexedDB and signed with your key. It does not travel anywhere yet because there is nowhere to: the Agora publishes no profile endpoint.
  • Measurements are not self-declared. They come from the public ledger, filtered by your pseudonym. If nothing there is signed by you, this shows zero — and says why.
