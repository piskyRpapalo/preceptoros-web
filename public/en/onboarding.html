1What is PreceptorOS?

Your AI on your machine, no cloud required. Persistent memory that belongs to you, and a Privacy Gateway that sanitises context before it reaches any external AI. No accounts, no telemetry.

2Your privacy, your control

The filter is the one that crosses out what is private. Paste your context and see what comes out before you hand it to anyone: it happens in your browser, nothing to install, and it also tells you where it does NOT reach.

4Your pairing code

Your identity is generated in the browser and the private key never leaves it. This pairing code is derived from your public key: always the same for you, and it carries no secret inside.

—

This code IDENTIFIES you, it does not authenticate you: anyone who knows your public key can compute it. Real linking will also require a signature, and that arrives with the backend.
