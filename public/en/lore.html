Where each thing comes from

  • The five quotations are literal and checkable on your own: they come from LORE.md, the material the product uses when you ask for the lector filter. They are here exactly as they are written there, unsummarised and never passed through a model — which is the only thing that guarantees what you read is what somebody reviewed.
  • The museum is a thread, not a fact. The rooms, the door and the walk were written by us to link the five pieces together. The pieces are checkable history; the corridor between them is literature, and separating the two is something we owe you.
  • The five plates are the application's own icons — Memory, Projects, Border, Profile and The Path — on violet-veined marble, not redrawn. Together they weigh 36 KB.
  • This slideshow uses no JavaScript. It is links and :target; each room has its own address and shares like any link. With JavaScript disabled, it still works.
  • None of this blocks installation. The skip button is at the top from the first moment, not at the end.
