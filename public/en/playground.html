Front page · Benchmark · Install

When you ask a cloud AI for help you have to hand it your context, and that is where paths, IPs and keys leak. Paste here what you were about to paste there, and see what comes out. In your browser, with nothing installed.

How it works, and what it does not promise

  • The rules are the product's. This filter is GENERATED from PreceptorOS's, not copied by hand. Verified by running the corpus's 36 cases through both implementations: same verdict on all 36, no surviving fragment.
  • It does not leave your browser. The text is filtered right here. This page has no network call that could carry it away, and you can check that in your browser's tools.
  • It is not infallible, which is why it says where it stops. Deterministic and auditable, with declared gaps: emails, Windows and macOS paths, URLs with a credential in the path, and «token» with a name in front. Always review before pasting.
