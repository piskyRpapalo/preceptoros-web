The full Test Book

Which model runs how fast, on which machine, and who measured it. Every row is a signed line carrying the sha256 of the model file: without that hash, «llama-3.2-3b» identifies nothing.

Where this table comes from
  • Nobody uploads anything by accident. The data is sent by your PreceptorOS from your machine, and only if you ask it to. Installing publishes nothing.
  • The hash and the signature are the data. A figure without the model's sha256 cannot be reproduced, and without a signature it cannot be attributed. Both columns exist so the table can be checked, not decorated.
  • Gaps are declared. When an engine does not say how many tokens it generated, the cell reads NO_DATA. It is not filled in with an estimate.
