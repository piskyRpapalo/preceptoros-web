Move the levers and watch the prompt being written below. The raw text is always visible and always editable: it is what the file carries, not what the dropdowns say.

What this page does, and what it does not

  • Nothing leaves your device. The agent is compiled in your browser and the file is saved from there. This page talks to no server.
  • The raw text wins. If you edit the text by hand, the levers stop overwriting it and you are told on screen. Losing your work by moving a dropdown would be the opposite of what this panel teaches.
  • The file carries what you see. agente.json stores the prompt exactly as it stands in the box, and records whether you edited it by hand.
